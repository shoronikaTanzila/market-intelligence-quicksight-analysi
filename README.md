# 📊 E-Commerce Market Intelligence & Tiered Loyalty Optimization

An end-to-end Market Intelligence workflow built within **Amazon Quick (QuickSight)** using Agentic AI, structured customer cohort datasets, and external market research benchmarks.

---

## 🎯 Executive Overview
This project evaluates 350 segmented e-commerce customer profiles to identify Lifetime Value (LTV) growth drivers, assess the margin impact of promotional discounts, and deliver leadership-ready retention strategies.

* **Tooling:** Amazon Quick Spaces, SPICE Engine, Agentic Q Assistant, Quick Research
* **Dataset:** E-Commerce Customer Behavior Dataset (350 records)
* **Author:** Tanzila Ahmmed

---

## 💡 Key Findings & Market Insights

1. **Gold Tier Anchors Core Revenue:**
   * Gold accounts average **$1,311.14** across **17.62 items** with an outstanding **4.68/5.0** rating—generating nearly 3x the revenue of Bronze members.
2. **The Blanket Discount Paradox (Margin Erosion):**
   * Non-discounted transactions average **$903.49**, while discounted transactions average **$787.27** (a **~13% decline** in basket size without volume expansion).
3. **High-Readiness Silver Upgrade Runway:**
   * Silver members average **$748.43** across **11.66 items**, offering the highest conversion ROI to Gold status.
4. **Critical Bronze Retention Risk:**
   * Bronze accounts display a **31.6-day latency gap** and **0% satisfied ratings** (50.9% unsatisfied), driven by onboarding friction rather than price.
5. **Geographic Concentration:**
   * 100% of Gold members reside in coastal hubs (San Francisco & New York).

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Shift to Threshold Discounting:** Eliminate blanket sitewide discounts and mandate order-value gates (e.g., *"$100 off on orders over $950"*).
2. **Silver-to-Gold Accelerator:** Trigger exclusive perks when Silver accounts cross $850 in cumulative spend.
3. **Automate Bronze Onboarding:** Deploy automated satisfaction check-ins and support workflows within 14–20 days post-purchase.
